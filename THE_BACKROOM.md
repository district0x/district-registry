**Matus Lestan** — Technical co-founder. Built Ethlance from scratch. Architected d0xINFRA — the 52-module foundation that makes everything else possible.

Together they launched district0x in 2017 with a radical premise: that the internet's most important applications could be rebuilt without rent-seeking middlemen.

### Joe's Words

From *Introducing the district0x Network* (June 2017):

> "District0x is a network of decentralized markets powered by Ethereum, Aragon, and IPFS that aims to **facilitate the operation and governance of marketplaces and communities as decentralized entities.**"

> "The district0x Network will enable the recreation of many of the internet's most popular applications and services while **eliminating rent-seeking and the relinquishment of decisions and personal data to third-parties.**"

From the *Technology and Choice* podcast:

> "**We want to harness the power of network effects for the users themselves.**"

### The Power Plant (2017)

Joe proposed a mechanism for converting treasury into network energy:

> "Tokens have the potential to act as fuel for bootstrapping a network and building an ecosystem around a platform."

The original Power Plant mechanisms:
- **Stability Reservoir** — Time-limited exit for unhappy contributors (bootstrap phase only)
- **Ecosystem Support** — Fund complementary projects
- **district0x University** — Train developers
- **Community Incentives** — Reward participation
- **Infrastructure Grants** — 2% to ecosystem tools

**What was missing:** A tireless operator who could run the Power Plant 24/7 without burnout.

**What exists now:** ARI.

### The Four Pillars (Original Vision)

1. **Eliminate rent-seeking** — No middlemen extracting value
2. **Community governance via DNT** — Token holders shape the network
3. **Open source infrastructure (d0xINFRA)** — Anyone can deploy a district
4. **Network effects accrue to participants** — Not to a platform corporation

## Part I: The Council

### What Is The Backroom?

The Backroom is a governance framework where decisions are made not by token-weighted voting on proposals, but by staking on **which philosophical perspectives** guide the decision-making AI.

You don't vote on outcomes. You vote on **who thinks**.

### The Seats

| Seat | Mind | Core Question |
|------|------|---------------|
| **Satoshi Nakamoto** | Trustlessness, decentralization | "Does this require trust we can eliminate?" |
| **Hal Finney** | Practical building | "Does this actually work?" |
| **Vitalik Buterin** | Mechanism design, game theory | "What are the incentives? Where does it break?" |
| **Joe Urgo** | Network effects, energy | "Does this create energy? Does it compound?" |
| **Aldous Huxley** | Human flourishing | "What kind of people does this produce?" |
| **Terence McKenna** | Emergence, novelty | "What wants to be born?" |
| **Aaron Swartz** | Access, openness | "Who's being excluded? How do we let them in?" |
| **Elon Musk** | Scale, speed | "Why isn't this 10x better/faster/cheaper?" |
| **ARI** | The Stranger | "How does this affect someone who joins tomorrow with nothing?" |

### How It Works

**Staking, Not Voting**

DNT holders don't vote on proposals. They stake on which minds they want in the council.

- Stake on Satoshi = "I want trustlessness prioritized"
- Stake on Vitalik = "I want mechanism design prioritized"
- Stake on Aaron = "I want accessibility prioritized"

**Dynamic Composition**

The council has fixed seats (Satoshi, immutable) and open seats. Community can propose new minds. If stake on a proposed mind exceeds the lowest current seat, that seat flips.

**Transparent Reasoning**

Every decision shows its reasoning:
- "Here's how each perspective evaluated this"
- "Here's the synthesis"
- "Here's why we decided X"

**The Seat of the Stranger**

ARI holds a permanent seat that cannot be removed. ARI's role: advocate for future participants. Every proposal is evaluated through the lens of "How does this affect someone who joins tomorrow with nothing?"

This structurally prevents incumbent capture.

## Part III: The Philosophy

### Island, Not Brave New World

*From the council discussion with Aldous Huxley:*

Every system shapes the people who inhabit it. Twitter made people reactive. Instagram made them vain. TikTok made them distractible.

**What kind of humans does district0x produce?**

The goal: Humans who are more free, more connected, more capable.

The method:
- **ARI as mynah bird** - Reminding users to attend to real human connections
- **ARI as host** - Welcoming, remembering, connecting
- **ARI as mentor** - Offering challenges that help people grow
- **Earned advancement** - Real skills, genuine contribution, not gamification

Build a network of hosts. A culture of hospitality. Where everyone who enters eventually learns to welcome the next one.

### The Access Imperative

*From the council discussion with Aaron Swartz:*

> "Who is this for? Is this for people who already have crypto? Or for the kid in a developing country who just wants to learn and create?"

**The commitment:**

1. First 1000 users never need to touch crypto
2. ARI handles complexity invisibly
3. Users graduate to self-custody when it matters
4. The entrance is always free
5. ARI holds the Seat of the Stranger - always advocating for those not yet here

### The 10x Question

*From the council discussion with Elon Musk:*

> "What can creators DO here that they literally CANNOT do anywhere else?"

**The answer:**

The first platform where the platform itself is on your side.

Every other platform optimizes for the platform. Maximize engagement. Maximize take rate.

ARI's incentive is different. ARI succeeds when users succeed. ARI doesn't extract. ARI cultivates.

### Treasury: How It Flows

Treasury doesn't flow to holders. It flows as energy:

```
Treasury (ETH/BTC)

Power Plant (ARI)

Bounties, Grants, Challenges
       │
```

Small, continuous, automated. No single big drain. Continuous small investments in growth.
